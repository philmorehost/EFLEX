# CodeCanyon Inspired Marketplace: Feature List

This document outlines the core features of the marketplace, divided into buyer-facing and seller-facing functionalities.

## Buyer Features

### 1. User Account Management
- **Registration:** Buyers can create an account using their email and a password.
- **Login/Logout:** Secure login and logout functionality.
- **Profile Management:** Buyers can view and edit their profile information, including their name and password.

### 2. Product Discovery
- **Homepage:** A curated homepage showcasing featured products, new releases, and popular items.
- **Search:** A powerful search bar that allows users to find products by keywords. The search will be implemented with AJAX for real-time results.
- **Filtering:** Users can filter products by category, price, rating, and other relevant attributes.
- **Categories:** Products will be organized into clear and intuitive categories for easy browsing.

### 3. Product Page
- **Detailed Information:** A comprehensive product page with a description, feature list, and technical requirements.
- **Image Gallery:** Multiple screenshots and a main product image.
- **Live Preview:** A link to a live demo of the product.
- **Pricing and Licensing:** Clear pricing information and different license options.
- **Reviews and Ratings:** A section for user-submitted reviews and ratings.

### 4. Purchasing and Checkout
- **Shopping Cart:** A simple and intuitive shopping cart to add and manage products.
- **Payment Integration:** Secure checkout process with Paystack and Flutterwave integration.
- **Virtual Bank Account:** Generation of a temporary virtual bank account for each transaction to automate payment confirmation.
- **Order Confirmation:** An email confirmation after a successful purchase.

### 5. Post-Purchase
- **Order History:** A dedicated section to view past orders and download invoices.
- **Product Downloads:** The ability to download purchased product files and license keys.

## Seller Features

### 1. User Account Management
- **Seller Registration:** A separate registration process for sellers, potentially requiring more information for verification.
- **Login/Logout:** Secure login and logout functionality.

### 2. Seller Dashboard
- **Sales Analytics:** A comprehensive dashboard with an overview of total earnings, sales volume, and product performance.
- **Earnings Report:** Detailed reports on monthly earnings and individual transaction records.

### 3. Product Management
- **Product Upload:** An intuitive form to upload new products, including title, description, category, tags, pricing, and product files.
- **Edit Products:** The ability to edit and update existing product listings.
- **Disable/Enable Products:** Sellers can temporarily delist their products.

### 4. Payouts
- **Payout Settings:** A section to connect a bank account for receiving payments.
- **Automated Payouts:** After the commission is deducted, the remaining amount is automatically transferred to the seller's account.

## Admin Features (Future Scope)

- **User Management:** Admin panel to manage all users (buyers and sellers).
- **Product Management:** Admin can view and manage all products on the platform.
- **Category Management:** Admin can add, edit, and delete product categories.
- **Commission Settings:** Admin can set the commission rate for sales.
- **Payout Management:** Admin can view and manage all payouts to sellers.
